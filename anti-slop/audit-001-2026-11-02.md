## Findings List

### Priority: HIGH (Hard Gate Violations)

1. **[Finding #01] R-02 — Copywriting: Forbidden Em Dash (`—`)**
   - **Location**: `src/components/FeaturesPortal.jsx:54`
   - **Reason**: The sentence contains an em dash: `"Generator nomor surat otomatis—tidak ada lagi tabrakan nomor urut antar divisi"`. R-02 strictly forbids em dashes in generated UI copy.
   - **Proposed Fix**: Replace with parentheses or comma: `"Generator nomor surat otomatis (tidak ada lagi tabrakan nomor urut antar divisi)"`.

2. **[Finding #02] R-17 / R-36 — Data & Numbers: Unverified Statistic Metric**
   - **Location**: `src/components/Hero.jsx:195-201`
   - **Reason**: Displays `"352 ++ Jumlah Kader Menggunakan TeraDesk"` without a verified source or honest data flag. R-17 strictly forbids hardcoding promotional metrics unless verified or flagged.
   - **Proposed Fix**: Replace with honest organizational profile data or label as `[REAL DATA / KADER AKTIF]` or replace with a factual statement (e.g. status kepengurusan aktif).

3. **[Finding #03] R-26 — Interactive Elements: Generic Root Social Links**
   - **Location**: `src/components/Footer.jsx:64, 76, 88`
   - **Reason**: Links point to generic domain homepages (`instagram.com`, `github.com`, `linkedin.com`) without actual organizational channel slugs.
   - **Proposed Fix**: Update with the real TERAMEDIA social media handles (e.g., `https://instagram.com/teramedia_poltek` or similar) or remove inactive external links to prevent dead/generic controls.

4. **[Finding #04] R-18 & R-26 — Orphaned / Fabricated Testimonial Component**
   - **Location**: `src/components/Testimonials.jsx`
   - **Reason**: Component contains hardcoded testimonial quotes ("M. Ishak", "Ketua Umum") but is orphaned and unused in `App.jsx`.
   - **Proposed Fix**: Either remove the unused file or verify testimonials with real quotes before integrating.

---

### Priority: MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate Violations)

5. **[Finding #05] R-07 — Background: Unjustified Grid/Stripe Patterns**
   - **Location**: `src/components/Hero.jsx:8` and `src/components/FeaturesPortal.jsx:247`
   - **Reason**: Thin vertical stripe grid (`bg-[linear-gradient(to_right,#1b2b52_1px,transparent_1px)]`) is applied as a default "tech" background pattern without documented brand purpose.
   - **Proposed Fix**: Remove the artificial background grid lines or tone down to a clean, solid deep-navy backdrop.

6. **[Finding #06] R-13 — Glow & Orbs: Heavy Radial Blur Glow**
   - **Location**: `src/components/Hero.jsx:11` and `src/components/FeaturesPortal.jsx:248`
   - **Reason**: Radial gradient orbs (`blur-[140px]`, `blur-[120px]`) behind headline text create the stereotypical AI floating glow effect.
   - **Proposed Fix**: Clean up the radial blur or replace with a purposeful subtle linear background gradient.

7. **[Finding #07] R-08 — Button Arrows: Ubiquitous Arrow Decorations**
   - **Location**: `src/components/Hero.jsx:71`, `src/components/Features.jsx:116, 151`, `src/components/Footer.jsx:36`
   - **Reason**: Arrow characters (`➔`, `>`) are automatically appended to buttons as pure decoration rather than directional purpose.
   - **Proposed Fix**: Remove redundant decorative arrows from standard buttons and secondary links; reserve directional arrows only for actual forward-stepping action workflows.

8. **[Finding #08] R-09 — Badges: Capsule Eyebrow Badges Above Headlines**
   - **Location**: `src/components/Hero.jsx:50` (`TERAMEDIA Prototype`) and `src/components/Features.jsx:70` (`Fitur Unggulan`)
   - **Reason**: Pill-shaped capsule badges parked directly above titles create the default AI landing page aesthetic.
   - **Proposed Fix**: Integrate the category context into the section typography hierarchy cleanly without floating pill capsules.

9. **[Finding #09] R-10 — Glassmorphism: Excessive Backdrop Blur Stacking**
   - **Location**: `src/components/Hero.jsx:84` and `src/components/FeaturesPortal.jsx:223`
   - **Reason**: Concurrently applying `backdrop-blur-md` on navbar, multiple dashboard cards, and modals violates the dose cap (maximum 1-2 elements).
   - **Proposed Fix**: Use solid opaque surface colors (`bg-[#0d1633]` / `bg-[#060D25]`) with crisp borders instead of stacking blur filters everywhere.

10. **[Finding #10] R-04 — Generic AI Icons: Decorative Sparkle/Zap Glyphs**
    - **Location**: `src/components/FeaturesPortal.jsx:254` (`Zap`), `src/components/CtaBanner.jsx:13` (`Sparkles`)
    - **Reason**: `Zap` and `Sparkles` are classic default AI glyphs used without content-specific relevance.
    - **Proposed Fix**: Replace with content-relevant organizational icons (e.g. `Layers`, `Shield`, `Activity`) or clean text.
